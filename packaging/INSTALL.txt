Synth Cook Book (Studio 87)

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and open SynthCookBook-RecipeBook.html in your web browser.

Files:
- SynthCookBook-RecipeBook.html  the recipe book: 14 synth panels (Behringer, Moog, Arturia, Korg)
- SynthCookBook-PatchBook.html   the older simple patch book (works offline, patches included)
- patches/                 the same patches as separate .json files
- SynthCookBook-manual.pdf       the manual
- PATCH-FORMAT.md          the patch file format
- SHARING.md               how to share your patches

To remove: delete the folder.
